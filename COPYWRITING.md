---

# Brand Voice

Appmist speaks in a way that is:

* Simple
* Friendly
* Professional
* Calm
* Trustworthy
* Clear
* Helpful

We avoid:

* Buzzwords
* Marketing hype
* Exaggerated claims
* Technical jargon
* Long paragraphs

Every sentence should be easy to read and understand.

---

# Writing Principles

Always:

* Write in plain English.
* Keep sentences short.
* Focus on benefits before features.
* Explain why something matters.
* Sound confident without exaggerating.
* Write for everyday Android users.

Avoid:

"Best app ever"

"Revolutionary"

"World-class"

"Game-changing"

"Industry-leading"

Instead describe the actual value.

# FAQ

## Why does the app need Accessibility Service?

Accessibility Service is used only to automatically lock the screen when the timer expires.

It is never used to collect personal information or monitor activity.

---

## Does TimeUp Lock collect my data?

No.

TimeUp Lock does not collect, store, or sell personal information.

---

## Does the app require an account?

No.

You can start using TimeUp Lock immediately.

---

## Can I choose any timer duration?

Yes.

Use the built-in presets or create a custom timer.

---

## Does the timer continue in the background?

Yes.

The timer keeps running even if another app is open.
