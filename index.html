TimeUp Lock

TimeUp Lock is a simple parental control and phone handoff app. Set a timer before handing over your phone, and when time is up, the device locks automatically until the parent unlocks it.

Current app TimeUp Lock
Lightweight Android app

No arguments. No complicated setup. Just peace of mind.

Parents can set a timer before giving their phone to a child or another person. When the selected time expires, the phone automatically locks and requires the parent to unlock it.

  • Quick timer presets for fast phone handoffs
  • Custom duration support when you need more flexibility
  • Background operation that keeps the timer running reliably
  • Parent protection through normal device authentication

Why TimeUp Lock

A simpler way to avoid screen-time arguments

Children often ask for "just five more minutes." TimeUp Lock helps avoid those difficult conversations by automatically locking the phone when the agreed time ends.

Without TimeUp Lock
  • Parents must keep reminding children that time is almost up.
  • The agreed time can easily stretch longer than expected.
  • Handing over a phone can end with frustration and repeated back-and-forth.
  • There is no simple automatic handoff moment.
With TimeUp Lock
  • Set a timer before handing over the phone.
  • The timer continues running in the background.
  • The screen locks automatically when time expires.
  • The parent unlocks the device using normal authentication.
