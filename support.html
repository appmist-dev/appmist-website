Troubleshooting and permission help

If TimeUp Lock is not working as expected, check the items below first.

Troubleshooting Guide

  • Make sure the timer was started successfully before handing over the phone.
  • Confirm that required permissions were granted.
  • Check that the app is allowed to keep running in the background.
  • Restart the app and try the timer again if something seems stuck.

Accessibility Service Help

If automatic locking does not work, verify that Accessibility Service is enabled for TimeUp Lock in your Android accessibility settings.

Notification Permission Help

Notification permission helps the app show timer status and time-up reminders. If you do not see timer updates, check whether notifications are allowed for the app.

Foreground Service Help

TimeUp Lock uses Foreground Service to keep the timer reliable in the background. If your device aggressively stops apps, review battery optimization settings that may affect background use.

Bug reports and feature requests

Clear reports help Appmist improve the app faster and more accurately.

How to Report Bugs

  • Describe what you expected to happen.
  • Describe what actually happened.
  • Include your Android device model if possible.
  • Include your Android version if possible.
  • Include the steps needed to reproduce the issue.

Feature Requests

If you have an idea that could make TimeUp Lock more useful, email your suggestion to appmist.in@gmail.com. Helpful requests explain the problem you want to solve, not just the feature name.
